Our Story

Who We Are

IUEA Journals is the scholarly publishing platform of the International University of East Africa. We are committed to advancing academic excellence through high-quality journals, books, and scholarly resources that uphold credible editorial and publishing standards.

Our mission is to support researchers, educators, institutions, and professional communities in disseminating impactful knowledge to a global audience. Through ethical publishing, peer review, editorial support, and open discoverability, we help authors extend the reach and value of their scholarly work.
