Author Guidance

Everything you need to prepare, submit, and publish your manuscript with IUEA Journals.

Manuscript Preparation

Use the journal's official template. Include a structured abstract (150–250 words), 4–6 keywords, and follow the referencing style of the target journal.

Submission

Submit through the online submission form. Ensure your manuscript is anonymized for double-blind peer review where applicable.

Peer Review

Submissions undergo editorial screening followed by double-blind peer review. Typical decision time is 4–8 weeks.

Revisions

Address reviewer comments carefully and return a revised manuscript with a point-by-point response letter.

Publication Ethics

Authors must ensure originality, disclose conflicts of interest, and comply with COPE guidelines. Plagiarism screening is applied to all submissions.

Open Access

All IUEA journals are open access. Published articles are freely available to readers worldwide, maximizing the reach of your research.

IUEA Journals is the scholarly publishing platform of the International University of East Africa, dedicated to advancing academic excellence through open access research.
