Open Access Policy

Our Commitment

IUEA Journals is fully committed to open access publishing. All published articles are immediately and permanently free to read, download, and share worldwide.

Licensing

Articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0), permitting reuse with attribution to the author(s).

Publication Fees

IUEA Journals does not charge submission fees. Article processing charges, where applicable, are transparently communicated before acceptance.

Archiving

Published content is preserved to ensure long-term availability.

IUEA Journals is the scholarly publishing platform of the International University of East Africa, dedicated to advancing academic excellence through open access research.
