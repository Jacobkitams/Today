Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, institutional affiliation, and manuscript files when you submit to IUEA Journals or contact us.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process manuscript submissions and manage peer review
  • To communicate with you about your submissions and enquiries
  • To improve our publishing services

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Manuscript and reviewer data are shared only with editorial staff and assigned reviewers as required for the peer-review process.

4. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting journals@iuea.ac.ug.

IUEA Journals is the scholarly publishing platform of the International University of East Africa, dedicated to advancing academic excellence through open access research.
